🔖 Grab Product Info — bookmarklet

One click on any Amazon product page grabs the title, price, and image, builds your affiliate link, and copies a ready-to-paste row to your clipboard.

Step 1 — Enter your Associate tag

The part after tag= in any SiteStripe link, e.g. yourtag-21.

Step 2 — Install it

Drag this button up to your browser's bookmarks bar:

📋 Grab Product Info
Don't see a bookmarks bar? Press Ctrl+Shift+B (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+B (Mac) to show it.

Step 3 — Use it

Open any Amazon product page → click the bookmark → it copies one tab-separated row to your clipboard → paste into column B of your Google Sheet.
